03.01 / Leadership

Head Prefect

As one of two Head Prefects, I lead the prefect team, support boarding students, mediate conflicts, and help connect students with faculty. The role has taught me that trust must come before authority.

03.03 / Leadership

Writing Center Tutor

As one of seven student tutors, I help peers make their essays complete, clear, and easy to follow by asking what they want to improve and guiding them through their own revisions.

03.04 / Leadership

Calligraphy Club

I founded a 30-member Calligraphy Club that teaches brush technique through monthly practice and invites the wider school community to write 福 for Chinese New Year.

03.02 / Leadership

Head Ambassador

As Head Ambassador, I lead campus tours, train new ambassadors, and help prospective families understand what it feels like to live and learn at Cheshire Academy.

03.05 / Leadership

Assistant Coach

After thirteen years as a competitive figure skater, I returned to the ice as an assistant coach, breaking down skills and helping younger skaters build confidence one step at a time.

03.06 / Leadership

Volunteer Teaching

In Guangxi, I adapted English lessons for second graders by replacing vocabulary lists with songs, games, and drawings that made students confident enough to speak.

03.07 / Leadership

Cooking Club

As co-founder of a 60-member Cooking Club, I help students cook together and share food during the International Food Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival, and Chinese New Year.
