Hello, I'mEleanor (Xiyue)Zhang

Self-intro

I grew up in Shenzhen, a city where new ideas seem to emerge around every corner. Growing up surrounded by innovation made me curious about how people turn ordinary observations into something meaningful.

That curiosity has followed me into my own projects. Whether developing a student reuse platform, researching what makes people trust a product, or creating bilingual content for my school, I enjoy understanding what people need and finding thoughtful ways to connect ideas with people.

Living away from home has also shaped another side of me. I know what it feels like to be new, uncertain, and far from everything familiar. Those experiences have made me more independent, but also more attentive to others. As a Head Prefect, student ambassador, and writing tutor, I try to offer the same encouragement and sense of belonging that once helped me find my confidence.

I'm especially drawn to marketing, communication, and entrepreneurship because they bring together what interests me most: human behavior, creative thinking, and the possibility of making everyday life a little better.
