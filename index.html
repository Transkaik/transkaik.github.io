Hello, I'mEleanorZhang.

Short bio

Building with curiosity,
leading with care.

I'm drawn to problems that sit between people, systems, and everyday life. At Cheshire Academy, I lead as Head Prefect, help international students feel at home, and build projects—from campus storage and secondhand exchange to tools that make cooking simpler. Photography teaches me to slow down and notice; figure skating taught me how to keep returning after a fall, even after injury changed my relationship with the sport. Across leadership, entrepreneurship, and visual storytelling, I care most about making unfamiliar spaces easier to enter and useful ideas easier to share. I'm still exploring how thoughtful design, culture, and communication can turn observation into something that genuinely helps people.
